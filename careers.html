Work with MatkaNumber

We are a small independent site looking for people who can make numbers clear. Roles are remote and project-based. Paid assignments are funded by sponsors and supporters and are confirmed in writing before work starts.

Maths and probability writer

Plain-language lessons of 600 to 1,000 words with worked examples.

Hindi and regional translator

Translate tools and lessons into Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu or Bengali.

Front-end developer

Build new browser tools in plain JavaScript. Accessibility matters here.

Video creator

Short explainers on chance, draws and number facts for YouTube.

Community and outreach

Bring the tools to teachers, clubs and creators who run draws.

Puzzle setter

Write the monthly number puzzle and its solution notes.

Apply

We read every application and reply to those that fit a current need. Your details are used only for recruitment.
