*Last updated: 12 November 2026*

## Summary

- The tools run in your browser. Names, lists and numbers you type into a tool are **not sent to us**.
- We receive personal details only when you submit a form.
- Advertising on this site is provided by Google AdSense, which uses cookies.

## Information you give us

When you submit a form (newsletter and Draw Kit, contact, contest entry, pledge, advertising enquiry or job application) we receive what you typed, the page you sent it from and the first page you visited. Forms are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit, a form-to-email service that processes the submission on our behalf. We use the details only for the purpose of that form, do not sell them, and delete them on request.

## Stored in your browser

We use your browser's local storage to remember that you dismissed the cookie notice and which page you first landed on. This stays on your device.

## Advertising and cookies

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites.
- Google's advertising cookies enable it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
- You can opt out of personalised advertising at [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads). You can also opt out of some other vendors' use of cookies at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- More on how Google uses information from sites that use its services: [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through a Google-certified consent message before personalised ads are shown.

## Embedded videos

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. YouTube may then set cookies under its own policy.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security under GitHub's privacy statement.

## Children

The site is intended for a general audience. The contest is open to adults only, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Your choices

You can ask for a copy of your details, or for correction or deletion, through the [contact page]({{ '/contact.html' | relative_url }}). Choose the topic "Privacy request".

## Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above.
