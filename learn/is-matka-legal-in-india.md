**Short answer: no.** Betting on matka draws is unlawful across almost all of India, offline and online. This page summarises the main rules in plain language. It is general information, not legal advice, and state rules differ.

## The old central law

The **Public Gambling Act, 1867** makes it an offence to run or to be found in a "common gaming house". It carves out games of "mere skill", but a numbers draw is pure chance, so the exception does not apply.

## State laws

Gambling is a state subject under the Constitution, so each state has its own act or has adopted the central one. Maharashtra, where matka grew up, has its own Prevention of Gambling Act, and police there have brought matka cases under it for decades. A few states license specific activities such as casinos or state-run paper lotteries. A licensed state lottery is a different thing from a private matka draw.

## The 2025 online gaming law

Parliament passed the **Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025**, which received the President's assent on 22 August 2025. In outline:

- It bans "online money games", meaning online games played with stakes for a monetary return, whether they are games of chance or of skill.
- It also targets advertising such games and processing payments for them.
- It applies to services offered from outside India to users in India.
- It promotes e-sports and social games that involve no stakes.

A website or app that takes money on matka numbers falls squarely inside the ban.

## What about "result", "chart" and "guessing" websites?

Many sites claim to only publish results or tips. They exist to serve an unlawful betting trade, they frequently link to betting operators, and advertising networks generally refuse to work with them. MatkaNumber is not one of them: we publish no results, charts or tips.

## What is clearly fine

- Drawing names or numbers for a **free** giveaway or a classroom activity.
- Learning the probability behind draws.
- Using random numbers for games with no stakes.

## If you are already losing money

Unlawful operators offer no protection: there is nobody to complain to when a payout is refused. Stopping is the only reliable fix. See [help for gambling problems]({{ '/learn/help-for-gambling-problems.html' | relative_url }}).
