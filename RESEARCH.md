# Research: MatkaNumber.com

## The decision
"Matka number" is overwhelmingly a Satta Matka (illegal numbers betting) search. Three ways to use the domain:

| Option | Traffic | AdSense | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Results / charts / guessing site | Highest | Not eligible: AdSense bars gambling content without certification, and India's Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 bans online money games and their promotion | Rejected |
| Pottery / clay pot shop content | Tiny | Eligible | Wastes the name |
| **Fair-draw tools + number library + probability education** | Captures tool searches ("random number generator", "name picker") and the informational side of matka searches ("is matka legal", "what is matka") | Eligible | **Chosen** |

Revenue lines: AdSense on tool and number pages (high repeat use), tool and email sponsorships, contest prize partners, branded draws, supporter contributions, and domain/partnership enquiries.

## Gap in the market
Almost none of the tool sites capture leads: most have no email form at all. MatkaNumber puts a lead band on every page (Fair Draw Kit + weekly email), a qualified advertising form (budget, timing, interest) and contest entry opt-in.

## Facts relied on
- Matka history (cotton rates, 1961 change, Worli 1962, New Worli 1964, decline): Wikipedia, "Matka gambling".
- Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025: assent 22 August 2025; bans online money games regardless of skill or chance, with advertising and payment restrictions (Mondaq, Bar and Bench, Chambers summaries).
