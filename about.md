## The idea

Millions of people type "matka number" into a search box every month. Almost everything they find is built to take their money: result boards, "guessing" forums and paid tips for an unlawful betting game that pays less than its odds.

MatkaNumber takes the same two words back to their plain meaning. A *matka* is a clay pot. A *number* is a number. Put them together and you get one of the oldest fair ways to pick something at random, plus a good excuse to explain how chance actually works.

## What you will find here

- **Tools** for fair draws, random numbers, dice, coins and number facts. They run in your browser and upload nothing.
- **A number library** with a page for every number from 0 to 99.
- **Lessons** on probability, randomness and the arithmetic of betting, written for people who do not think of themselves as maths people.
- **Videos** from educators we trust.
- **A free monthly puzzle** with a public draw.

## What you will never find here

Gambling results, charts, tips, predictions, "lucky numbers" for sale, betting links or betting advertisers.

## Who it is for

Teachers and students, creators running giveaways, clubs and offices running draws, puzzle fans, and anyone who suspects the numbers game is rigged against them and wants to see the proof.

## How it is funded

Display advertising through Google AdSense, clearly labelled sponsorships, and voluntary contributions from readers. Advertisers have no say over what we publish. See [advertise]({{ '/advertise.html' | relative_url }}) and [support]({{ '/support.html' | relative_url }}).

## Editorial standards

Every calculation on the site can be checked by hand, and we show the method. Legal summaries name the laws they rely on and are reviewed when the law changes. If you spot an error, [tell us]({{ '/contact.html' | relative_url }}) and we will fix it.
