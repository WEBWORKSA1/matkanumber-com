## 1. Giveaway rules template

Copy this and replace the parts in brackets.

> **[Name of giveaway]: official rules**
>
> 1. **Organiser.** [Your name or organisation], [city, country].
> 2. **Who can enter.** Open to residents of [countries] aged [18] or over. Employees of the organiser and their immediate families cannot enter.
> 3. **How to enter.** [Describe the single action required]. Entry is free. No purchase or payment is needed.
> 4. **Entry period.** Opens [date, time, time zone] and closes [date, time, time zone]. Late entries are not counted.
> 5. **Limit.** [One] entry per person.
> 6. **Prize.** [Describe the prize and its approximate value]. No cash alternative.
> 7. **Draw.** [Number] winner(s) and [number] reserve(s) will be selected at random from all valid entries on [date] using the Matka Draw tool at MatkaNumber.com. The draw record will be published at [link].
> 8. **Notification.** Winners will be contacted by [method] within [3] days and must reply within [14] days, or a reserve will be contacted.
> 9. **Publicity and data.** The organiser will publish winners' [first name and city]. Entry details are used only to run this giveaway.
> 10. **General.** Void where prohibited. The organiser's decision is final. This giveaway is not sponsored or administered by any social media platform.

## 2. Ten-point fair draw checklist

- [ ] Rules published before entries opened
- [ ] Entry is free
- [ ] Closing time stated with a time zone
- [ ] Entry list frozen and saved at closing time
- [ ] Invalid and duplicate entries handled as the rules say
- [ ] Draw method named in advance
- [ ] Draw done once, with reserves drawn at the same time
- [ ] Draw recorded or done live for larger prizes
- [ ] Draw record published with the result
- [ ] Winners contacted privately, never asked to pay anything
