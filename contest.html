Rules

  1. Free entry. No purchase, payment or stake of any kind. This is a skill puzzle followed by a random draw among correct entries. It is not gambling.
  2. One entry per person per month. Entrants must be 18 or older.
  3. Entries close at the end of the last day of the month. Late entries are not counted.
  4. Winners are picked from correct answers using the Matka Draw tool, and the draw record is published on this page.
  5. Winners are contacted by email and have 14 days to reply, after which a reserve is drawn.
  6. Prizes are as described for each month, are not exchangeable for cash unless stated, and any taxes are the winner's responsibility.
  7. Void where prohibited by law. The organiser's decision is final. Your details are used only to run the contest, as set out in the privacy page.
