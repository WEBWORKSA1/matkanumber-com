What the checker tells you

Prime or composite. A prime has exactly two divisors, 1 and itself. Everything else above 1 is composite and can be written as a product of primes in exactly one way. That product is the prime factorisation.

Divisors. Every whole number that divides yours without a remainder. Numbers with many divisors, such as 12, 60 and 360, are useful for sharing and measuring, which is why we have 12 months, 60 minutes and 360 degrees.

Other bases. Binary uses only 0 and 1 and is how computers store numbers. Hexadecimal uses sixteen symbols and is a compact way to write binary.

For the numbers 0 to 99 there is a full page each in the number library.
