Two systems, one number

The international system groups digits in threes: thousand, million, billion. The Indian system groups the last three digits and then pairs: thousand, lakh, crore. So 12,345,678 in the international style is written 1,23,45,678 in the Indian style and read as one crore twenty-three lakh forty-five thousand six hundred seventy-eight.
