The two rules

Write symbols from largest to smallest and add them: VIII is 5 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 8. When a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, subtract it: IV is 4, IX is 9, XL is 40, XC is 90, CD is 400 and CM is 900. Only those six subtractive pairs are standard.

Roman numerals have no zero and no place value, which made arithmetic slow. The positional system with zero that came from India replaced them for calculation, and Roman numerals survive on clock faces, in book chapters and in film credits.
