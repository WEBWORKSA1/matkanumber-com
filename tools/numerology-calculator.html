How the numbers are worked out

Name number. In the Pythagorean method each letter gets a value from 1 to 9 (A is 1, B is 2, up to I at 9, then J starts again at 1). The values are added and the digits of the total are added again until one digit remains. The totals 11, 22 and 33 are traditionally left as they are.

Life path number. All digits of the birth date are added and reduced in the same way.

Entertainment only. Numerology is a tradition, not a science. There is no evidence that these numbers describe personality or predict events, and they cannot pick winning numbers for any draw. See numerology: fact or fiction.
